Regional Pune Macro Analysis

Pune Real Estate Macro Trends: East Pune (Hadapsar & Kharadi) vs West Pune (Wakad & Hinjewadi)

Comparing the investment dynamics of Pune’s twin economic corridors: East Pune’s BFSI and SEZ dominance versus West Pune’s technology highway corridor.

1. The Dual Engine of Pune Real Estate

Pune’s real estate expansion has historically operated along two high-performing axes: East Pune, anchored by EON IT Park Kharadi, Magarpatta City, and Hadapsar SEZ; and West Pune, powered by Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park at Hinjewadi, Baner, and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.

2. East Pune: Kharadi & Hadapsar Real Estate Dynamics

East Pune attracts enterprise banking and fintech institutions. Projects like Lodha Giardino in Kharadi cater to enterprise finance directors with lush garden-themed residences. Hadapsar features mature integrated townships like Magarpatta and Amanora, yet faces airport transit limits and road congestion along the Ahmednagar highway.

3. West Pune: Wakad & Hinjewadi’s Unrivaled Connectivity

West Pune commands higher investor interest from Mumbai and international NRI circles due to immediate access to the Mumbai-Bangalore Highway (NH-48) and the Mumbai-Pune Expressway. At Lodha Altero Wakad, travel time to Navi Mumbai and the upcoming Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMIA) is reduced to just 75 minutes.
