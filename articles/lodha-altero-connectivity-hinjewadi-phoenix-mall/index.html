2. PMRDA Metro Line 3 Multiplier Effect

The elevated 23.3 km Pune Metro Line 3 connects Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park directly to District Court (Shivajinagar). With Wakad Station located just 3 minutes from Lodha Altero, residents enjoy rapid transit across Pune Central without highway vehicular friction.

Within a 5-kilometre radius, residents have direct access to top-tier international educational institutions (Indira National School, EuroSchool, The Academy School) and multi-speciality tertiary hospitals (Jupiter Hospital Baner, Surya Mother & Child Care Hospital, Lifepoint Multispeciality).
