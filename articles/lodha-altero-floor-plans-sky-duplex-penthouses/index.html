Lodha Altero Floor Plans Guide: 3 BHK, 4 BHK, 5 BHK Sky Duplex & Penthouses

Detailed technical breakdown of carpet areas, three-side open ventilation, 10.5 ft ceiling clearances, and Mivan monolithic construction across three G+37 towers in Wakad, Pune.

1. Spatial Efficiency & 10.5 Ft Ceiling Volumes

Typical high-rise apartments in Pune feature standard 9.5-foot slab heights. Lodha Altero introduces 10.5 ft floor-to-ceiling clear heights, augmenting interior air circulation and creating a voluminous palatial ambiance. Monolithic Mivan aluminum formwork eliminates protruding columns inside living rooms and bedrooms, optimizing usable carpet area.

2. 3 BHK Grande & 3.5 BHK Royal Suite (with Study)

The 3 BHK Grande residences range from 1,185 to 1,396 sq.ft. carpet area. Featuring expansive 24-foot living-dining salons, dedicated walk-in wardrobe zones in the master suite, and private sundecks with glass balustrades. The 3.5 BHK Royal Suite (1,450 sq.ft.) adds a private work-from-home executive study room tailored for modern hybrid IT leaders.

3. 4 BHK Imperial Haven: Corner Sky Sanctuary

Measuring 1,559 to 2,105 sq.ft. carpet area, the 4 BHK Imperial residences occupy corner positions in Tower 3, boasting three-side open ventilation. Dual viewing balconies provide 270-degree panoramic outlooks over the Hinjewadi skyline and green riverfront contours.

4. 5 BHK Bespoke Sky Duplex & Penthouses (Levels 35–37)

The crown jewels of Lodha Altero are the limited-edition duplex penthouses on Levels 35 to 37. With 2,600 to 3,416 sq.ft. carpet area, they feature soaring double-height living salons, internal private staircases, expansive master retreats, and direct elevator access to the 25,000 sq.ft. rooftop sky club.
