The 25,000 Sq.Ft. Rooftop Sky Club: Pune’s Highest Elevated Leisure Deck

Suspended 120 metres in the sky, Lodha Altero's rooftop sanctuary redefines luxury residential living with temperature-controlled swimming, sports, and stargazing.

1. An Elevated Sanctuary Above Urban Dynamics

While standard residential developments in Wakad relegate amenities to ground level surrounded by driveway traffic, Lodha Altero elevates luxury to the pinnacle of its G+37 towers. Spanning 25,000 sq.ft. of uninterrupted rooftop space, the sky deck delivers clean airflow, zero ground acoustic disturbance, and unhindered horizon views of the Western Ghats.

2. 50-Metre Heated Infinity Sky Pool

The centerpiece of the 37th-floor club is a 50-metre, temperature-regulated heated infinity swimming pool. Engineered with acoustic dampening slabs and edge-overflow troughs, residents can swim year-round regardless of winter chill or monsoon mist while enjoying panoramic vistas over the Mula River.

3. Tournament Padel Court & 400m Jogging Track

For fitness enthusiasts, the rooftop includes an enclosed tournament-grade Padel tennis court and a 400-meter cushioned jogging track cushioned with specialized EPDM rubber flooring to minimize joint impact.

4. Computerized Stargazing Celestial Observatory

Positioned far above urban street illumination, the celestial observatory features a high-aperture computerized telescope and planetarium seating, providing children and families an enchanting window into the cosmos.
