The Lodha Pune Residential Ecosystem: Micro-Market Comparison & Flagship Lodha Altero Wakad

How Lodha Group has established landmark residential communities across West Pune, East Pune, and South Pune—anchored by the newly launched flagship sky sanctuary at Lodha Altero Wakad.

1. Lodha Altero (Wakad, West Pune) — The Flagship Sky Sanctuary

Situated behind Croma on the Wakad-Bhosari BRTS corridor, Lodha Altero represents Lodha’s most architecturally ambitious high-rise development in Pune. Rising across three G+37 storey towers with a 25,000 sq.ft. Rooftop Sky Club at 120 metres elevation, Lodha Altero delivers ultra-spacious 3 BHK, 3.5 BHK, 4 BHK, and bespoke 5 BHK Sky Penthouses starting from ₹2.09 Cr* (MahaRERA: P52100079692).

Its proximity to Phoenix Mall of the Millennium (4 mins), Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park Hinjewadi (10 mins), and the upcoming Wakad Metro Station makes it the premier choice for tech CXOs and high-net-worth investors.

2. Lodha Panache (Hinjewadi Phase 1, West Pune)

Designed specifically for IT professionals and corporate executives working inside Hinjewadi Phase 1, Lodha Panache features smart-engineered 2, 3 & 4 BHK residences with dedicated co-working lounges, 20,000 sq.ft. clubhouse, and Olympic sports amenities (MahaRERA: P52100050302).

3. Lodha Giardino (Kharadi, East Pune)

Catering to Pune’s East IT and BFSI corridor near EON Free Zone and World Trade Center (WTC), Lodha Giardino offers biophilic garden residences set within botanical landscaping and private sky terraces (MahaRERA: P52100047585).

4. Lodha Bella Vita (NIBM Road, South Pune)

Anchoring South Pune’s scenic green belt, Lodha Bella Vita is an Italianate Mediterranean-inspired hillside enclave offering resort-style living with pristine air quality and views of Anandvan reserve forest (MahaRERA: P52100046960).

5. Lodha Belmondo (Gahunje, Mumbai-Pune Expressway)

Spread across 100 sprawling riverside acres opposite the MCA Cricket Stadium, Lodha Belmondo features a 9-hole Greg Norman signature golf course, 50,000 sq.ft. clubhouse, and luxury riverside villas along the Pavana river promenade (MahaRERA: P52100000283).
