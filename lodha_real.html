Most Common Queries

Where is Lodha Altero located?

Lodha Altero, one of the upcoming projects in Wakad enjoys a prime location on the 36-metre-wide Wakad-Bhosari BRTS Road, just a 5-minute drive from the Mumbai-Pune Highway.

How well is Lodha Altero connected to other parts of the city?

This new property in Wakad boasts a central location with exceptional connectivity. Positioned on the 36-metre-wide Wakad-Bhosari BRTS Road, it is just a 5-minute drive from the Mumbai-Pune Highway. The location ensures seamless access to prominent locations like Hinjewadi and Balewadi, just 10-15 minutes away, and Nigdi, Pimpri and Pradhikaran, within a 15-minute drive.

What are the key commercial hubs that are easily accessible from Lodha Altero?

The location offers excellent connectivity and access to a well-developed social infrastructure, making it ideal for employees working in leading IT and technology companies in Hinjewadi and Balewadi. The Hinjewadi Wakad Bridge ensures swift connectivity to the Hinjewadi IT hub in approximately 10 minutes, while the upcoming Balewadi Bridge will seamlessly link Balewadi High Street to Wakad in around 10 minutes. Additionally, the industrial belt of PCMC, including Nigdi, Pimpri, and Pradhikaran, is just a 15-minute drive away.

What are the nearby entertainment hubs, healthcare centres, educational institutions and key landmarks around Lodha Altero?

Lodha Altero combines peaceful living with unparalleled convenience, offering effortless access to essential amenities and vibrant local attractions. Whether it's entertainment, healthcare, education or connectivity, the location ensures a well-rounded lifestyle.

  • Education: Esteemed institutions like Indira Institute of Management, NICMAR, National Insurance Academy, Sanskruti International School, and Euro School are within a 10-minute drive.
  • Healthcare: Leading hospitals such as Aditya Birla Hospital, Manipal Hospital, and Lifepoint Hospital are also just 10 minutes away.
  • Shopping and Entertainment: Premium destinations like Phoenix Mall of the Millennium, Westend Mall, E-Square, and Balewadi High Street are a quick 5-10 minute drive.
  • Hotels: Premium hotels like Hilton, Hyatt, and Vivanta are just 7–10 minutes away.
