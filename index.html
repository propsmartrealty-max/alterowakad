Frequently Asked Questions

Lodha Altero is situated on the 36-metre-wide Wakad-Bhosari BRTS road in Kaspate Wasti, Wakad, Pune (411057), conveniently located directly behind Croma Electronics. It is just 2 minutes from the Mumbai-Bangalore Highway (NH-48) and 10 minutes from Hinjewadi Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park.
The project offers spacious 3 BHK (1,185 – 1,396 sq.ft.), 3.5 BHK (1,450 sq.ft.), 4 BHK (1,559 – 2,105 sq.ft.), and bespoke 5 BHK Sky Penthouses (2,600 – 3,416 sq.ft.), each equipped with wide double-height viewing balconies and 10.5 ft floor-to-ceiling clear heights.
Lodha Altero features a dedicated 25,000 sq.ft. Rooftop Sanctuary on the 37th floor. Key attractions include a 50-meter heated infinity pool, professional tournament Padel Court, computerized stargazing observatory, 400m cushioned jogging loop, sunset amphitheatre, and café co-working pods.
The project is registered under MahaRERA registration number P52100079692. You can verify all sanctioned tower plans, floor plans, and RERA approvals at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.
Possession is scheduled in planned phases starting December 2028 with overall project completion targeted by June 2030 as registered with MahaRERA.
